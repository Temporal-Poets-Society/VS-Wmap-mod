Chart the world as you explore. World Map (Wmap) turns your world map and minimap into a nautical chart or a shaded terrain atlas. Follow coastlines, read the landscape, mark useful discoveries and carry your knowledge to other players on paper maps.

With Earth, zoom from whole continents down to a harbour and see distant terrain beyond your normal view distance. With Cylinder World, scroll continuously east or west across the world's seam. Wmap also works on its own, mapping the terrain you visit.

Development release. World Map is still in development. Feedback and bug reports are welcome, especially for the distant-terrain prototype and multiplayer use.

Documentation: the World Map wiki explains every feature, setting and command. Release and source: World Map 0.5.5 on GitHub. Report a problem: GitHub issues, or the comments here.

Worlds made before World Map 0.5.4 are not supported: start a new world when coming from 0.5.3 or earlier. Worlds from 0.5.4 carry on with 0.5.5.

Features

  • Two map styles: a parchment nautical chart with ink coastlines, contours, shallows and a compass rose, or a shaded terrain map with elevation and water depth. Earth supplies regional climate colours and mapped river courses.
  • Exploration: your survival chart fills in as you travel. With predicted terrain available, exploration follows what you can see over the landscape, accounting for the planet's curve. Otherwise, it charts a circle around you. In survival only the land you have seen reaches your game; creative mode shows the whole world.
  • Atlas and minimap: smooth zoom, a follow mode, player heading, waypoints and prospecting readings. Geographic worlds also show latitude and longitude, with an optional box in the top left corner (under the minimap when it sits there) that gives your coordinates, heading, local time and the world's clock (UTC). With Astra Terra for Earth the local time is the solar time its sky follows, so noon is when the sun stands highest.
  • Useful markers: place and edit the game's waypoints, filter prospecting readings by ore, and choose which marker layers to show. Optional automatic markers remember supported discoveries when you harvest them or look at them while sneaking.
  • Paper map sharing: draw a snapshot of your discoveries, hand it to another player and let them choose what to copy into their atlas.
  • Ore heat maps (Creative, off by default): a server can switch them on (OreHeatMaps in ModConfig/wmap.json) to colour the map by one ore, or by how many ores the prospecting pick would list, at every zoom, worked out once per world and stored. They follow the prospecting pick's own reading; where the game's own rock layers are used (Earth's default from 2.0.1, or worlds without Earth) a cell is known only where its chunk is loaded.
  • Distant horizons with Earth: predicted terrain extends beyond loaded chunks, up to 8,192 blocks by default. It is lit as the game lights its own land and wears the game's grass and leaf colours through the seasons (with Earth, each region's own wet and dry seasons), so it meets your loaded chunks without a pale ring. Relief, forest canopy, high ground by its climate and volcano craters show at a distance, and land you have seen is drawn as you saw it. In survival it shows the land in your line of sight and the land you have charted. The experimental mid-distance view adds previously recorded terrain, simplified buildings and distant lights.

Paper maps

Craft a blank map from eight parchments around an ink and quill in the center; the quill is kept. Hold the map and right-click to name it and choose what to include: charted areas, recorded terrain, waypoints, automatic markers and prospecting readings.

Another player can hold the drawn map and right-click to copy the parts they want. Their own recorded terrain takes priority, and duplicate waypoints and readings are skipped. A drawn map and a blank map in the crafting grid make another copy. Maps are snapshots of what you knew when you drew them.

Wind charts with Sea

With the optional Sea mod, carry a craftable Jonas barometer in your hotbar or backpack to see wind direction and strength worldwide on the atlas and minimap. Choose Weather now, Prevailing now, or January through December to inspect dominant winds throughout the year. These layers are available in survival while you carry the barometer, as well as in creative mode. On an Earth world the barometer also reads the air pressure where you stand and whether it is rising or falling: a falling glass comes before storms.

Getting started

  1. Install Wmap in your Mods folder. In multiplayer, install the same version on the server and every client.
  2. Enter a world and press M, your remapped world-map key, or type .wmap. Type .wmap vanilla to open the original map.
  3. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom and right-click to add or edit a waypoint. The atlas starts in Chart, centered on and following you at a local zoom. Use Whole world to zoom out, Follow to return to your position and Chart / Terrain to change style.
  4. The minimap keeps its usual F6 toggle and Ctrl+F6 corner control. Ctrl+Shift+V toggles the coordinates box (coordinates, heading, local time and UTC). Ctrl+Shift+F switches the far view on or off.

Built and development-tested with Vintage Story 1.22.7; the manifest minimum is 1.22.0. Checked with Earth 2.0.0 and 2.0.1, Cylinder World 1.1.1, Hydrology 0.6.2 and Sea 0.6.1. Earth, Cylinder World and Sea are optional for Wmap itself. Without Earth or Cylinder World, it uses recorded terrain. Cylinder World alone supplies an approximate continent map without real relief; distant terrain requires Earth. Fast Map is not required.

On worlds with a prediction provider, the server prepares a world chart in the background on first use. Allow time for it to finish (a minute or two); the chart is cached for later sessions. Creative mode shows the whole predicted world. In survival you see only the land you have seen; a server can give the whole map to chosen players with the wmap privilege, or to everyone with AllowAllPlayers.

Settings and servers

Under Customize → Earth, Wmap adds Wmap: explore by sight, on by default in new worlds. Restart the world or server after changing it. The Earth mod is not required for this Wmap setting.

The unexplored map is for creative only. In survival, the predicted terrain of land you have not seen never reaches your game: the server works out what each player has seen and sends the chart, close-up detail and distant terrain of that land only, so a modified client cannot uncover the rest. Creative players, players with the wmap privilege and servers with AllowAllPlayers get the whole predicted map.

Advanced settings in ModConfig/wmap.json control exploration, styles, automatic markers, paper maps, winds, distant terrain, ore heat maps and cache sizes; the wiki's Settings page lists every one. Set PredictTerrain to false to use recorded terrain with an unsupported terrain generator.

The Jonas barometer and blank map also appear in the shared Earth+ creative tab, below Everything.

Compatibility and development

Terra Prety and other terrain generators are not fully supported for prediction. Coexistence with Fast Map and other map replacements has not been play-tested. Automatic markers currently recognise supported base-game objects; coverage of other mods' resources is limited.

Distant terrain is a simplified view, and recorded buildings update when you visit again. It cannot reproduce overhangs, thin structures, moving ships or creatures. Seasonal polar sea ice is not yet shown by season.

Paper maps are available now. Sextant fixes, surveying, chart tables and route plotting remain planned features.

Report problems on GitHub issues or in the comments here. Include your game and mod versions, companion mods, map mode and style, relevant settings, a screenshot and a short reproduction, and the lines with [wmap] from the game's logs. For geographic problems, include latitude and longitude.
