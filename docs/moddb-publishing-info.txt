RELEASE NOTE (draft: everything since 0.5.1)
World Map 0.5.3 is a development release.
- The far view, with Earth: lit as the game lights its own land; the game's grass and leaf colours through
  the seasons (each region's own wet and dry seasons with a current Earth); no pale ring where your loaded
  chunks end; relief finer than the mesh; high ground coloured by its climate; volcano craters; land you have
  seen drawn as you saw it; forests at their recorded height, with crowns, meeting the nearer terraces
  without a line.
- The coordinates box sits in the top left corner on the game's own box colour and shows the local time and
  the world's clock (UTC); with Astra Terra for Earth the local time is the solar time its sky follows. On
  worlds without geography it shows the time alone. It draws under every dialog.
- With Earth, the Jonas barometer reads the air pressure where you stand and whether it is rising or falling.
- Ore heat maps on the Creative map (colour the map by one ore, or by how many ores the pick would list, at
  every zoom; stored per world; they agree with the pick) ship switched off: a server can turn them on with
  OreHeatMaps in wmap.json.
- A new Earth world on full GLO-90 heights no longer waits about two minutes for the climate colours.
- Big rivers no longer get a second, darker river beside them; grid labels follow the degree format.
Client and server need this same version.
