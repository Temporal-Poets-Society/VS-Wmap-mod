CHANGELOG FIELD (since 0.5.1, the version on the Mod DB)
World Map 0.5.4 (15 November 2026)
Start a new world: worlds made with earlier World Map versions are not supported. Client and server need 0.5.4.
- The unexplored map is for creative only. In survival, only the land you have seen reaches your game: the chart,
  the close-up detail and the far view. A modified game cannot uncover the rest. The same holds for spectators and in
  a single-player survival world. Creative players, the wmap privilege and AllowAllPlayers get the whole map.
- In survival the far view shows the land in your line of sight out to 8,192 blocks and the land you have charted;
  new land appears a few seconds after you see it.
- The world option "Wmap: shared world chart" is gone.
- Paper maps chart only what their drawer has seen.
- Sturdier with Earth and Cylinder World updates: World Map says in the log what it could not read, and far terrain
  says why it is not drawn.

World Map 0.5.3 (14 November 2026)
- The coordinates box sits in the top left corner in the game's own box colour, under any window you open. It shows
  the local time and the world's clock (UTC); with Astra Terra for Earth the local time is the solar time its sky
  follows. Ctrl+Shift+V shows or hides it.
- With Earth, the Jonas barometer reads the air pressure where you stand and whether it is rising or falling.
- The far view looks like the land beside it: lit as the game lights its own land, the game's grass and leaf colours
  through the seasons, no pale ring where your chunks end, finer relief, high ground coloured by its climate, forests
  at their height, volcano craters, and land you have seen at its real height.
- Ore heat maps on the Creative map: colour the map by one ore, or by how many ores the prospecting pick would list,
  at every zoom. They agree with the pick, and /wmapore compares a column with it. They ship switched off; a server
  turns them on with OreHeatMaps in wmap.json.
- With a mismatched version or an atlas that did not start, the atlas says why instead of waiting for ever.

World Map 0.5.2 (a test build, not uploaded before)
- A new Earth world on full GLO-90 heights no longer waits about two minutes for the climate colours.
- Big rivers no longer get a second, darker river beside them.
- The map grid's labels follow the degree format you choose.
