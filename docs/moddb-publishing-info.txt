RELEASE NOTE (draft: everything since 0.5.1)
World Map <version> is a development release.
- Ore heat maps on the Creative map: colour the map by one ore, or by how many ores the prospecting pick
  would list, at every zoom; stored per world. With Earth's regional ores they work anywhere on the planet.
- The coordinates box beside the minimap shows the local time; with Astra Terra for Earth it is the local
  solar time its sky follows. On worlds without geography the box shows the time alone.
- With Earth, the Jonas barometer reads the air pressure where you stand and whether it is rising or falling.
- A new Earth world on full GLO-90 heights no longer waits about two minutes for the climate colours.
- Big rivers no longer get a second, darker river beside them; grid labels follow the degree format.
Client and server need this same version.
